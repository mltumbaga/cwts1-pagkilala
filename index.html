My ICT Community at San Mariano National High School MAIN

I consider the ICT community at San Mariano National High School MAIN meaningful because I learned technical skills there, served as ICT Club President, and used what I learned to help teachers and fellow students. The community gave me opportunities to lead, share knowledge, and contribute through practical ICT service.

01 · People and Groups

The community included ICT Club officers and members, fellow students, teachers, and the school's ICT educators and adviser. As ICT Club President, I helped lead the club and support technology-related learning.

02 · Places and Institutions

Important spaces included the school's computer laboratory and ICT learning areas. The school, its teachers, ICT instructors, and the ICT Club provided the setting and resources for learning computer systems, troubleshooting, and office applications.

03 · Relationships and Practices

The community connected through ICT Club activities, classroom learning, hands-on troubleshooting, and helping teachers with computer and printer problems. I also shared Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint skills with fellow students as they prepared for work immersion, and guided classmates and clubmates in computer repair and troubleshooting.

04 · Assets and Strengths

The community's strengths included students' willingness to learn, shared technical knowledge, the computer laboratory, guidance from ICT teachers and the club adviser, and opportunities to practice computer systems servicing. I contributed by helping repair teachers' computers and printers, teaching office tools, and guiding peers through troubleshooting.

05 · Community Concerns

A challenge in a student ICT community can be that learners have different levels of experience and may need different amounts of guidance when learning technical tasks. I will include only difficulties I actually observed, rather than making unsupported claims about the whole school.

06 · My Contribution and Limitation

One contribution I make is organizing technical and research information so it is easier for the team to use. A limitation I need to recognize is that verifying difficult information can take time, especially when the available evidence is incomplete. I should be careful not to overstate what I have confirmed and should communicate uncertainty when a detail cannot be verified.

My Place in the Community

I was part of the ICT community at San Mariano National High School MAIN and served as ICT Club President. I helped guide clubmates and classmates in computer repair and troubleshooting, helped teachers with computer and printer issues, and taught fellow students office applications in preparation for work immersion. These experiences showed me how technical skills can support other people.

The portrait provides the context for my written reflection. The strengths, relationships, and concerns shown here help me examine what citizenship means in my own circumstances. My school ICT community gave me a setting in which to practice responsibility, honesty about what I can verify, and communication with others.
